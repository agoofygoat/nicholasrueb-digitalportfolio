A — Paper Craft

Subtle paper grain texture on backgrounds. Cards feel like cut paper with soft shadows. Dashed borders, hand-drawn accents. Warm, tactile, like a real workshop notebook.

Card Element

✦

B — Dark Workshop

Dark backgrounds with fine noise grain. Cards are frosted glass with warm kraft accents. Like a dimly lit maker space — moody but inviting. High contrast text.

Card Element
Grid paper + offset shadows

C — Graph Paper

Graph/grid paper texture as background. Cards have offset colored shadows (like stacked paper). Feels like an engineering sketchbook — structured but handmade.
